# CSM coaching

**Status: blocked.** Two gaps: speaker resolution, and no way to tell a CS call
from a sales call.

Load `starmesh-core`, then `person-lens`.

## Refuse, and say this
Calls are classified internal or external — not sales, CS, support or product.
So CS conversations can't be separated out, and CS behaviour can't be measured.
Coaching a CSM on sales-call metrics would be wrong.

Name both gaps: call function classification, and speaker resolution.

## Once both exist
Different benchmarks from sales — don't reuse `rep-coaching`'s:
- Proactive contact rate, not talk ratio
- Issue resolution time
- Renewal preparation lead time
- Expansion conversations opened
- Whether risk got escalated early enough

Same rule as rep-coaching: no feedback without a specific call to point at.
